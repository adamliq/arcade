    About this port

    In 1996 this preview shipped as BAM_DEMO.EXE, a DOS/4GW program that streamed a Smacker movie from CD and ran a small script for palette fades and music fades. This page does the same job in the browser. It reads the original BAMDEMOF movie and DEMOF.SCR script and decodes them in JavaScript. The original video and audio are not converted, and nothing needs installing.

    • A Smacker 2 decoder covers the Huffman trees, the four block types, palette deltas and DPCM audio. Its output matches ffmpeg on every frame, and the audio is bit-exact.
    • Fades are done the way the VGA hardware did them, by scaling the 6-bit palette. They are just smoothed to your display's refresh rate instead of stepping at 9 fps.
    • The music and effects tracks are mixed separately, so the script's music fade-in and fade-out work as written.
    • Scaling uses sharp bilinear filtering at the 4:3 shape a CRT gave it, with an optional scanline mask. You can seek and step frame by frame, and pick scenes from the list.
